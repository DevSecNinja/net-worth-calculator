# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project. Constructive behavior
includes using welcoming language, respecting different viewpoints and experiences, accepting
feedback, focusing on what is best for users and the community, and handling sensitive security or
privacy information responsibly.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, trolling, publishing another person's private information, or exposing a user's financial
data, vault, passphrase, or security report.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where
someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not include
unnecessary personal or financial information. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn,
temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter
or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
